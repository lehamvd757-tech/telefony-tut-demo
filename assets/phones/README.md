# Phone visual resources

Each SVG is an original, model-specific schematic sheet with front, rear, and side silhouettes. The selected color is applied to the model's enclosure at runtime; storage capacity does not affect the visual. The SVGs are visibly marked `СХЕМА · НЕ ФОТО` and are not photographs, official product renders, or manufacturer-approved artwork.

No licensed product photographs were available for this implementation. The diagrams are provisional references only; their geometry is simplified and must not be presented as precise product photography or a manufacturer rendering. They do not grant rights to the depicted industrial designs, product names, or trademarks.
